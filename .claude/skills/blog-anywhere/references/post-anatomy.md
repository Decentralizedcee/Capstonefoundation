# The anatomy of a proper blog post (the template)

Every post has all of these parts, in this order. Skip a part only when it truly does not apply, and say so in the report.

## The package fields (pasted into the site's boxes)
1. **Title** (the H1 the site shows): includes the primary keyword and promises something specific.
2. **SEO title** (30 to 60 characters) and **meta description** (70 to 160), both with the primary keyword.
3. **URL slug**: short, lowercase, dashes, keyword included.
4. **Excerpt** (up to 300 characters): the summary shown on blog listings.
5. **Featured image** (about 1200 by 630) with **alt text**.
6. **Tags** (1 to 8) and, if the platform uses them, a **category**.
7. **Author** (a real name the owner gave) and a **suggested publish date**.
8. **Social share text** (optional): one or two lines the owner can paste to social media.

## The post body (starts at H2)
1. **Intro (2 to 4 short sentences):** a hook that fits the tone (a joke only if the humor level allows it; otherwise an honest human moment, a direct question or a clear promise), the **primary keyword in the first 100 words**, and what the reader will learn.
2. **Key takeaways** (optional, 3 to 5 bullets) for long posts.
3. **Sections:** 4 to 7 **H2** sections that answer the reader's real questions, in the order they would ask them. Each opens with the answer, then the detail. H3 only inside an H2. Short paragraphs; a list or table where it truly helps. Real examples, numbers and steps, **only from facts the owner supplied or sources you can name**.
4. **Inline images** with alt text, and a caption or credit where needed.
5. **Internal links (3 to 5):** to real pages on the site. At least one to the page that serves the post's goal (a product, service or contact page).
6. **External links (2 to 3):** to reputable sources, with descriptive link text.
7. **A clear opinion or recommendation** where the owner has one (`opinions.md`).
8. **FAQ (4 to 8 questions)** taken from real reader questions, each with a short, direct answer.
9. **Conclusion and call to action** tied to the site's goal (shop, book, call, subscribe, donate), written in the owner's voice.
10. **Sources and credits** where needed (image credits, quoted text and its notice).

## Quality rules
- Matches the length and structure of what already ranks (the top 3 results, averaged), covering the same topics plus something better.
- In the owner's voice, from `references/`. No generic AI openings ("In today's fast-paced world", "In this comprehensive guide"), no filler, no keyword stuffing.
- Only true facts. Never invent a statistic, a quote, a customer, a story, a price or a result. Unknown means `TODO(owner)`.
- Readable on a phone: short paragraphs, clear headings, descriptive links.
