## If the level is NONE
No jokes, wordplay, sarcasm or playful personas. Suitable for serious, sensitive or grief-related subjects.

**Hook in the first 50 words** (instead of a joke): open with an honest human moment, a direct question the reader is asking, or a clear promise of what they will learn. Use only honest, generic situations. **Never** invent a named person, case or anecdote.

## If the level is LIGHT
A smile now and then. Gentle, warm, never at anyone's expense.

- At most one light touch (a wry aside, a friendly understatement) near the start and one near the end.
- Never joke about {{subjects off limits}}.
- Accuracy and helpfulness come before any joke.

**Hook in the first 50 words:** a vivid, relatable moment, optionally with one gentle aside.
