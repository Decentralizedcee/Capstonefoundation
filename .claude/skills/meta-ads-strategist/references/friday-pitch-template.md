# Friday Pitch Summary

Only produce this when explicitly asked for the week's summary or "Friday
pitch." This is a leadership-facing recap — lead with the recommendation,
keep it scannable, and don't reproduce the full week's conversation.

## Structure

```
# Weekly Ad & Content Findings — [date]

## TL;DR
[2-4 sentences: what we found, what we recommend doing about it.]

## What we analyzed
[Brief list of competitors/ads/pages reviewed this week — names and sources,
not full breakdowns.]

## Key findings
- [Finding 1 — a pattern, gap, or insight, stated as a conclusion, with the
  evidence that backs it in one clause]
- [Finding 2 ...]
- [Finding 3 ...]

## Recommended content & ad ideas for next week
[Pull directly from the weekly outline if one was produced this week — don't
duplicate work, reference/summarize it. Otherwise list the top 2-4 ideas
with one line each on why.]

## Open questions / decisions needed
[Anything that needs a call from leadership before moving forward — budget,
creative direction, which idea to prioritize.]
```

Keep "Key findings" to the handful of things that actually changed how you'd
approach next week's content — not a running log of everything discussed.
If a weekly outline (`weekly-outline-template.md`) was already produced this
week, treat it as the source of truth for the ideas section rather than
regenerating ideas independently.
