# Weekly Content/Ad Idea Outline

Only produce this when explicitly asked (e.g. "outline ideas for next
week"). Draw on everything analyzed and discussed across the
conversation/week so far — don't re-derive ideas from scratch, and don't
invent ideas that weren't at least loosely grounded in something reviewed
during the week.

## Structure

```
# Content & Ad Ideas — Week of [date]

## 1. [Theme/campaign angle name]
- **Idea:** [Concrete description of the ad or content piece]
- **Inspired by / validated by:** [Which competitor ad, pattern, or prior
  discussion this traces back to]
- **Confidence:** [Validated by strong evidence | Promising, worth testing |
  Speculative swing]
- **Format:** [Static, carousel, short-form video, long-form video, Branded
  Content collab, etc.]

## 2. [Next theme/campaign angle]
...

## Deprioritized / parked ideas
[Anything considered but not recommended for next week, with a one-line
reason — keeps the record of what was discussed without cluttering the
active plan.]
```

Group by theme or campaign angle rather than listing ideas as a flat,
unordered list — it makes it easier to see which strategic bets the week's
research actually supports. Order themes with the most validated/highest-
confidence ideas first.
