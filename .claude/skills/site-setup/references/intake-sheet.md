# Intake sheet

Ask **one group at a time** in plain, friendly words. Give a short example with each question. Wait for the answers. Any question can be answered "I don't know" or "later": record `unknown` and carry it as `TODO(owner)`. **Never guess.** Never ask for a key or password.

If the owner supplied a build spec, read it first and **ask only what it does not answer**.

---

## A. The business or organisation
1. Name (exactly as it should appear)?
2. In one sentence: what do you do, and for whom?
3. Type: local service business, nonprofit or organisation, professional services or portfolio, shop, other?
4. How long have you been going (year started)?
5. What is the main goal of the website? (calls, forms, bookings, donations, sales, sign-ups)

## B. What you offer
6. List every service, program or product you want a page for.
7. Do you want prices or price ranges shown? If yes, what are they?
8. Guarantees, licences, certifications, registration numbers you are happy to publish?

## C. Places
9. Which cities or areas do you serve (or "online")?
10. Do you have a public address? Should it be shown on the site?

## D. Contact
11. Phone, email, opening hours, exactly as they should appear everywhere.
12. Social media accounts (links).

## E. Your audience
13. Who is your ideal customer, donor, client or member? What worries them?
14. What are the top 10 questions people ask you? (These become blog topics.)

## F. Proof (only what is true and allowed)
15. Review count and rating; best testimonials. **Does each person agree to be quoted by name?**
16. Real numbers you can state (years, people helped, jobs done, response time). Only what you can stand behind.
17. Two or three true stories from real work. **Is it OK to share them? With names, or anonymous?**

## G. Brand and design
18. Logo, colours, fonts, photos you already have?
19. Websites whose look you like? Or do you have a written build spec or design document?
20. Words or feelings the site should give (calm, bold, warm, premium, practical)?

## H. Voice
21. How should the writing sound? Pick words: warm, direct, formal, playful, inspirational, practical, gentle...
22. Humor level: **none**, **light** (a smile now and then) or **playful** (jokes welcome)? Any subjects where there must be no humor at all?
23. Words, phrases or topics that must never appear?
24. Can you share a few things you have written (emails, posts, notes) so the writing sounds like you? Or a writing sample whose style you love?

## I. Special content rules
25. Privacy: anything that must never be published (money amounts, names, children's details, case details, locations)?
26. Faith, legal, medical or financial wording we must handle carefully?
27. Will posts quote any text (for example Scripture, laws, standards)? If so, which version or source?

## J. Pages and structure
28. Which pages do you need? (Home, About, services or programs, contact, donate, blog...)
29. Do you want a **blog**? Do you need **service + location pages** (usually only for local service businesses)?
30. Is any page copy already written and fixed (must stay word for word)?

## K. Images and video
31. Do you have your own photos? Are they ready, with consent from everyone who appears?
32. Should we also use free stock photos (Unsplash) when needed? Any rules by context? (For example: "own photos for anything about our work; stock only elsewhere.")
33. Any videos on YouTube or Vimeo to embed? (Send the links.)

## L. Setup
34. Do you own a domain? Which one?
35. Do you have GitHub and Render accounts (or are you happy to create them)?
36. A Google account to use for Search Console, Business Profile and Analytics?
37. Do you have, or will you get, a keyword tool (Semrush or similar)?
38. Which framework: **Astro** or **Next.js**? (Not sure? Say so and I will recommend one.)

## M. Search goals
39. Three competitors or sites you would like to outrank?
40. Any seasonal dates or events that matter (for example awareness days, peak seasons)?

---

After the last group, **read back a summary** of what you recorded (with every `unknown` listed) and ask the owner to confirm or correct it. Save it to `docs/intake.md`.
