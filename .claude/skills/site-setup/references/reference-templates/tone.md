# Tone (template)

## Overall feel
{{e.g. "Friendly neighbour who happens to be the expert: warm, direct, never salesy."}}

## Tone by content type
| Type | Tone |
|---|---|
| Blog posts | {{playful, conversational, informative}} |
| Service pages | {{confident, reassuring, clear, action-focused}} |
| Emergency / urgent topics | {{calm and practical, jokes dialled way down}} |
| Pricing / cost topics | {{honest, transparent, specific}} |

## Always
- Answer the question first, joke second.
- Give specifics (numbers, timeframes, steps).
- Treat the reader as smart but busy.

## Never
- Talk down to the reader.
- Make jokes at the expense of customers in distress.
- Overpromise ("guaranteed", "best in the city") without proof.
- Sound like a brochure.
