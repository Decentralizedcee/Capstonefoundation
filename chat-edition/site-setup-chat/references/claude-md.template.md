## 6. SEO rules (every page)

Full list: `docs/on-page-seo-checklist.md`. Essentials:

- Exactly **one H1** with the primary keyword.
- **Meta title** (≤ 60 characters) and **meta description** (≤ 160 characters) unique per page.
- Canonical URL, Open Graph and Twitter tags.
- Primary keyword in the **first 100 words**.
- **3 to 5 internal links** and **2 to 3 external links** per blog post.
- **4 to 8 FAQ questions** per blog post, with FAQPage JSON-LD.
- JSON-LD: `Organization` or `LocalBusiness` site-wide, `Article` on posts, `Service` on service pages, `BreadcrumbList`.
- Clean lowercase hyphenated URLs containing the primary keyword.
- Lighthouse target: {{100 | the owner's or spec's number, for example 95+}} on mobile for performance, accessibility, best practices and SEO, checked on the built site.
- Do not submit to Search Console or request indexing until the real domain is live and indexing is on.
