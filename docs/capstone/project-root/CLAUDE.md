# Capstone Foundation: Website Project

A Christian charity helping widows and orphans in Enugu and Abuja, Nigeria. Simple, beautiful, fast, mobile-first, static website. Published first on Render at a temporary address; the domain `capstonefoundation.net` is connected later.

## 0. Source of truth and precedence

1. **`docs/capstone-build-spec.pdf`** is the full build specification. It is **canonical for all copy, content, routes, design tokens and acceptance criteria.** Read it fully before doing anything. Use the page copy **word for word**.
2. **This file** adds project conventions and resolves the few conflicts found when the spec was cross-checked (section 9). If this file and the spec disagree on copy or design, **the spec wins**, except where section 9 says otherwise.
3. **`docs/cross-check.md`** lists open decisions for the owner. Do not decide those yourself.
4. If something is not covered anywhere, **ask the owner before adding it.**

## 1. Ground rules (from the spec)

- Use only facts in the spec. **Never invent** statistics, names, dates, addresses, quotes, testimonials, partners or Scripture. Missing information uses the placeholder system (`src/config/site.ts` flags: `showFounding`, `showTrustees`, `showRegistration`, `showAddress`, `bankDetails`, `sportProgramUrl`; sections depending on a missing item stay hidden with a TODO comment).
- **Voice:** inspirational, hopeful, openly Christian. Short sentences, plain words. Always "we", never "the foundation says". Scripture is an invitation, not a hammer. **Layered with "sparkle text"** (vivid, memorable, emotionally true language, used sparingly) for Stories and any new copy. See `references/voice.md` and `references/sparkle-text.md`. **Fixed page copy from the spec stays word for word and is never sparkled.**
- **Privacy:** never publish money amounts, receipts, bank screenshots, diagnoses, case details or children's surnames. Young people appear by first name only. People appear in photos only with consent.
- **Do not build** the payment back end, database, user accounts or payment gateway. Prepare for them only as the spec describes (a `server/` folder with a README, and the `DonationPanel` component reading `PUBLIC_API_URL`).
- Never put a secret in the repository or the browser.

## 5. SEO rules for this site

- Titles and headings naturally include "Nigeria", "Enugu" or "Abuja". Other organisations use the name Capstone Foundation, so location must always be clear.
- Unique title and meta description per page, canonical URLs, Open Graph and Twitter tags, `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, RSS for stories, JSON-LD `Organization` (name, logo, contact, `sameAs`) on every page.
- Lighthouse **95 or higher on mobile** for performance, accessibility, best practices and SEO, checked on the built site.
- **The SEO method (`docs/seo-step-by-step-guide.pdf`) applies here through the `blog` skill only:** stories are the topical-authority engine. There are no service + city pages. Do not run `service-page`.
- Do **not** submit to Search Console or request indexing until the real domain is live and `PUBLIC_INDEXABLE` is `true`.

## 6. Images

There are two sources, and the rule depends on **what the page or story is about**.

**A. The owner's own photos** (`images-incoming/` → `scripts/process-images.mjs` → `src/assets/images/` → `astro:assets`).
**Required** for anything that shows or describes **Capstone's own work**: programs, outreaches, beneficiaries, testimonials, projects, events, the team, the About and Get involved pages, and any story that is about Capstone's own work (for example "How we choose who to help"). People in these contexts come **only** from the owner's photos, with consent.

**B. Unsplash**, whenever it is needed and the context allows:
- **Stories on general topics** (Bible and church topics, how to support a grieving widow, International Widows' Day, mentorship, sponsoring school fees, and the like): Unsplash photos are welcome, **including photos of people**, because the photo illustrates a general topic and not Capstone's work.
- **Pages or stories about Capstone's own work:** Unsplash may be used only for **atmosphere with no people** (landscape, sunrise, open Bible, fabric, bread, a building).
- **Never** caption, alt-text or place an Unsplash photo so that it looks like Capstone's own beneficiaries, team, events or testimonials. Never use Unsplash for a testimonial. Alt text for an Unsplash photo describes what is visible as a general illustration (never "a Capstone widow").

**C. Placeholder.** A flat `#EBEBEB` block captioned "Photo to come" until a slot is filled.

Unsplash technical rules (from Unsplash's API guidelines): the key lives only in `.env` and a local script (`scripts/fetch-unsplash.mjs`), never in the repository or the browser; use the URLs the API returns (**hotlink with a plain `<img>`, never through `astro:assets`**); trigger the download endpoint when a photo is chosen; credit the photographer and Unsplash with links **beside the image** and in the footer "Photo credits" line. Demo apps are limited to 50 API requests an hour. Show the owner every Unsplash photo chosen (id, description, link) in the report.

Own-photo pipeline: sharp, max 2000px on the long side, AVIF and WebP, strips all EXIF including GPS, records each image in `src/data/images.ts` (id, alt, caption, page, slot, credit). It **skips and reports** receipts, bank screenshots, IDs, chat screenshots, medical documents, school-fee slips and anything showing an account number. Alt text describes only what is visible, with no names unless the owner gives them. No videos in the repository (host on YouTube or Vimeo and embed, if the owner asks). Propose a **slot map** (home hero, About, one per program page, Get involved) and **wait for approval** before placing any own photo.

## 7. Scripture

**Translation: NIV** (owner decision, 5 Nov 2026) for all new Scripture quotations, including in Stories.

- **Quote exactly and verify.** Fetch the passage from an authoritative NIV source (BibleGateway NIV or Biblica) and copy it exactly. Never quote from memory. If it cannot be verified, write `TODO(owner): verify the verse text (NIV)`. Never present a paraphrase as a quotation.
- **Cite** every quotation as book chapter:verse and "(NIV)".
- **Keep quotations short.** Biblica permits up to 500 verses without written permission, provided they are not a complete book of the Bible and not 25% or more of the total text of the work. Stay far below that.
- **Copyright notice.** Wherever NIV text appears, include the notice Biblica requires (confirm the exact wording at biblica.com/permissions). The standard form is: "Scripture quotations taken from the Holy Bible, New International Version®, NIV®. Copyright © 1973, 1978, 1984, 2011 by Biblica, Inc.® Used by permission. All rights reserved worldwide." Put it in the footer and on any page or story that quotes NIV.
- **Verified NIV text** (BibleGateway, 5 Nov 2026):
  - Psalm 68:5: "A father to the fatherless, a defender of widows, is God in his holy dwelling."
  - James 1:27: "Religion that God our Father accepts as pure and faultless is this: to look after orphans and widows in their distress and to keep oneself from being polluted by the world."
- **Open question for the owner (do not decide alone):** the spec's fixed copy quotes Psalm 68:5 as "Father of the fatherless and protector of widows" (wording that matches the ESV, not the NIV) and describes James 1:27 as "visit orphans and widows in their affliction" (a paraphrase, not NIV wording). Until the owner decides, leave the fixed copy exactly as the spec has it, and ask at Stage 3 whether to switch those two passages to the NIV text above so the whole site uses one translation.

## 8. Working method (gates)

1. **Stage 1: folders.** Check the environment, create the folder tree, verify it, then **stop** so the owner can add logos and photos.
2. **Stage 2: check uploads.** List what arrived; flag problems; do not process yet.
3. **Stage 3: plan.** Reply with the file tree, the component list and questions. **Wait for approval.**
4. **Stage 4: build,** in the spec's order: scaffold and config; design tokens and layout; Home; About; Programs; Get involved; Donate and Contact; Stories; SEO and structured data; final checks. After each step run the build and report in two or three sentences what is done and what is next. Stop at the slot map.
5. **Stage 5: acceptance** against the spec's "Done means" list.
6. **Stage 6: SEO skill.** Only when the owner says so.

The owner is not technical. Explain each step in plain words before doing it. Never ask the owner to paste a key into the chat.
