# Humor: Capstone Foundation

**Humor level: none.**

This site serves widows and orphans. Jokes, dad jokes, asides meant to amuse and playful "guy at the pub" voices are **off**. This overrides the default humor rules in the SEO kit.

## What replaces the "hook in the first 50 words"
Open every post with a **human moment or a clear promise**, in plain words. One of:
- A real, relatable situation the reader is in ("Someone you love has just lost her husband. You want to help, and you are afraid of saying the wrong thing.")
- A direct, honest question the reader is asking ("What does James 1:27 actually ask of us?")
- A short, concrete statement of what the reader will learn.

Use only situations that are honest and generic. **Never** invent a specific named person, case or anecdote. Stories about real people come only from `stories.md` and only with consent.

## Lightness that is allowed
- Warmth, gentleness and hope.
- Plain, human wording.
- Occasional encouragement ("You can start small.").

## Hard limits
- No jokes about grief, illness, poverty, death, widowhood, orphanhood or faith.
- No sarcasm.
