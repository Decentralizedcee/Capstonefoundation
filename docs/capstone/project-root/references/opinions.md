## What we believe (from About: Why faith and Values)
- We serve because of **Christ**. Scripture calls God the "Father of the fatherless and protector of widows" (Psalm 68:5) and tells us to visit orphans and widows in their affliction (James 1:27).
- **Love is best seen in action.** Every act of help is also an invitation to know Christ more closely.
- We do this work with **prayer, honesty and respect** for every person we meet.
- **Help should leave a person's honour intact:** "a gift and a next step, not a handout."
- We aim for **independence through skills, savings and school, not dependence.**
- **Follow-up matters more than programmes.** We keep showing up.
- We go to people **where they are, and listen before we help.**
- **Integrity:** every naira is accounted for, and we report openly to those who give.
- Help goes where it is **most needed**, and "the quietest voice is not overlooked."
- Every program is **rooted in prayer and care for the whole person.**
