## Programs
- **Widows Empowerment, Voice of the Widows (VOW) Forum:** started early 2026; more than 20 widows; meets twice a month in Abuja; reaches widows in other states online; more than half of members have received support; one widow was set up in a grinding-machine business this year; skills training in tailoring, baking and catering is planned for next year.
- **Scholarships:** school-fees sponsorship and vocational training for orphans and teenagers; 3 students sponsored; young people are also learning vocational skills. Applicant records a short video explaining their situation and shares the child's school results; Capstone assesses and looks for funding.
- **Healthcare support:** help with medical costs for those who cannot afford treatment; each case assessed for seriousness and urgency; applicant records a short video. No case details, ever.
- **Sport Development Program:** soccer and basketball for teenagers, with mentorship, counselling and skills; in-person sessions, with mentoring and learning online; launching soon, backed by Capstone Foundation. No prices.

## How people reach us for help
Two ways: they come to us with a need, or we find them through outreaches and home visits. Every case is assessed for how serious and how urgent it is before we act.
