# Tone: Capstone Foundation

## Overall feel
Editorial and restrained, like a well-made annual report. Hopeful, warm and steady. The page speaks through plain words, space and honest detail, not decoration or drama.

## Tone by content type
| Type | Tone |
|---|---|
| Stories (blog): Bible and church topics | Thoughtful, encouraging, practical. Warm teacher, not preacher. |
| Stories: practical how-to (supporting a widow, sponsoring school fees, starting a ministry) | Clear, caring, step by step, honest about what is hard. |
| Stories: awareness (International Widows' Day) | Informed and compassionate. Facts first. No guilt. |
| Stories: how we work (how we choose who to help) | Transparent, accountable, calm. |
| Donate and Get involved | Inviting and sincere. Never pushy. |

## Sparkle level (see `sparkle-text.md`)
| Content | Level |
|---|---|
| Stories | Moderate: 2 to 4 sparkle moments per post (opening, turning point, closing) |
| Donate, Get involved (new copy only) | Light: one line at most |
| Fixed spec copy | None. Word for word. |

## Always
- Lead with the reader's need or question, then answer it plainly.
- Treat every person in a story with dignity and honour.
- Be specific and practical.
- Include a gentle, genuine invitation to pray, give, volunteer or partner where it fits.
- Make the location clear (Nigeria, Enugu, Abuja) where relevant.

## Never
- Humor at anyone's expense, or jokes about loss, grief, illness, poverty or orphanhood.
- Sensational or emotionally manipulative storytelling.
- Overpromising results.
- Talking down to the reader.
