# Voice: Capstone Foundation

Source: the Capstone build spec, plus the owner's instructions of 5 Nov 2026 and the owner's sparkle-text style reference. Nothing is invented.

## Who is speaking
- **We**, the Capstone Foundation team: a Christian charity serving widows and orphans in Enugu and Abuja, Nigeria.
- Always **"we"**. Never "the foundation says" or "Capstone Foundation believes".
- Speaking to: people who might give, volunteer, partner or pray; churches, businesses, organisations; widows and families seeking help.

## Two layers of voice

1. **The spec voice (always).** Inspirational, hopeful, openly Christian; short sentences; plain words; "we".
2. **The sparkle layer (Stories and new copy, sparingly).** Vivid, memorable, emotionally true lines that make a reader pause, taken from the owner's reference on "sparkle text". The owner's words: *"This is how I would like most of the content to sound, especially the blog posts."* Full guide and guardrails: **`references/sparkle-text.md`**. Fixed spec copy never gets this layer.

## How we sound
- **Inspirational, hopeful, openly Christian.**
- Short sentences. Plain words.
- Scripture is **an invitation, not a hammer.**
- Warm, direct, respectful. We say plainly that we serve because of Christ, and we never pressure anyone.
- Quietly poetic in a few chosen places: a fresh image, a line with rhythm, a feeling put into plain words (see `sparkle-text.md`). The rest of the page is clear and plain.
- We speak about people with dignity: "a gift and a next step, not a handout."

## Phrases that are genuinely ours (from the site copy)
- "A firm foundation for widows and orphans."
- "Every life stands on a foundation, and loss can shake it overnight."
- "We stand with widows and orphans in the practical things that shape their future."
- "Every program is rooted in prayer and care for the whole person."
- "Stand with us."
- "Follow-up matters more than programmes. We keep showing up."
- "Help should leave a person's honour intact."

## Never
- Generic AI openings ("In today's fast-paced world", "In this comprehensive guide").
- Guilt, shock, pity-baiting or "poverty porn" language.
- Money amounts, receipts, bank details in stories, diagnoses or case details.
- Children's surnames. Young people appear by first name only.
- Invented statistics, names, dates, quotes, testimonials, partners or Scripture.
- Preachy lecturing. Invite, do not scold.
- Clichés ("beacon of hope", "tapestry", "journey", "shining light") and ornate, over-written sentences.
- Imagery of war, horror or drama for its own sake. Sparkle stays hopeful and grounded.

## Spelling
The site copy mixes "programs" (site text) and "programmes" (Values). Use **"programs"** in new copy for consistency with the page copy, unless the owner says otherwise.
