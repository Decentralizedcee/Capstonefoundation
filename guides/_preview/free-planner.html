Questions, answered simply.

Is it really free?
Yes. The planner is free when you join our email list. There is nothing to buy.
What will you do with my email?
We will send you occasional letters from Une Femme Divine. You can unsubscribe at any time, and we never share your email with anyone.
Is my planner information private?
Yes. Your dates, moods and notes stay in your own browser, on your own device. We never see them.
Is this medical advice?
No. The planner shares lifestyle and self-care inspiration only. The seasons are estimates drawn from the dates you enter, and it must not be used to avoid or plan a pregnancy. If something feels unusual or worries you, please speak with a doctor.
Do I get an online planner or a PDF?
Both. The online planner is interactive, with a calendar and calendar export. The PDF is made for printing or filling in on your screen.
