Good to know09

Questions, answered simply.

What is Divine Elixir?

A daily collagen ritual, created for women. A carefully composed formula, halal and made in Europe. We present it as a moment of self-care, never as a quick fix.

How do I take it?

One scoop a day, stirred into 200–300 ml of water, juice or your morning smoothie until fully dissolved. Pregnant, breastfeeding or taking medication? Consult your doctor before use.

Where is it made, and is it halal?

Manufactured in Slovakia, in the European Union, under European quality standards. Yes, it is halal. The collagen is bovine and there is no pork.

Who created Une Femme Divine?

Two entrepreneurs in Dubai who wanted one daily ritual made truly for women, after a full year of development with a laboratory. Our webshop opened on 29 September 2026.

What results can I expect?

We do not promise beauty or health results. Divine Elixir is a daily ritual and a moment for yourself. How it feels to you is what matters most to us.
