More than a group.

Une Femme Divine is not just a product. It is a movement back to self. The WhatsApp community is where that movement becomes a conversation: small, warm and real.

Who we are speaking to

She starts her morning with ritual. She is intelligent, graceful and grounded. She gives a lot to others: her work, her family, her dreams. Une Femme Divine is the moment she gives back to herself.

What it is not

  • Not a sales channel
  • Not medical advice
  • Not a place for comparison, diets or pressure

What success looks like

Women reply, share and feel safe. The group becomes the closest circle around the brand, and a natural, never pushed, path to our email list and our shop.

How it is set up03

Two spaces, a few simple rules.

Space one

Announcements

Admins only. News, schedules and the monthly moments. Quiet, so nothing important gets buried.

Space two

The Circle

The chat group where the women talk, reply to prompts and share their rituals.

Posting principles

  • Five to six posts a week. Fewer, better posts. Saturday stays empty on purpose.
  • Ask more than we broadcast. About four in five posts invite a reply.
  • Products rarely. At most one Divine Elixir mention in five posts. The free guides appear once a month.
  • Reply within 24 hours. Every woman who shares something is thanked.
  • Two admins post, so the voice stays consistent.

Voice

CalmWarmShort sentences“You” and “we”Never loud

Emojis stay soft and sparse: a white heart, a leaf, a cup, a candle. At most one or two per post.

Timing

Times in this plan are CET. 08:30 CET is 11:30 in Dubai and 07:30 in the UK, so morning posts work across our markets. WhatsApp does not schedule posts by itself, so admins set a phone reminder, or use a scheduling tool, and send each post on time.

Shaping the brand together09

Her voice helps build ours.

One brand moment each week. They are how the women influence what Une Femme Divine says, shares and makes.

Week 1

You decide

A poll on something real, such as which topics to share first. The next week we say what won: “You said, we did.”

Week 2

Divine Voices

With her permission, we share one woman’s words or ritual, with her first name only. We always ask first.

Week 3

Ask the founders

Admins collect three questions, and the founders answer in a voice note of two minutes or less. It lets the women hear who is behind the brand.

Week 4

Free guides

One gentle post about our free guides, Your Divine Ritual and Rhythm of the Body, with the link to join the email list. Then nothing more that month.

Always

  • Ask permission before using anything a woman writes in a post, a card or an ad.
  • Say thank you in the group, in public, in our calm voice.
  • Share what we learn from the group with the whole team each month.

Guardrails10

Safe, kind and honest.

We always

  • Share lifestyle and self-care inspiration
  • Say “this is not medical advice” when health comes up
  • Welcome every background, faith and way of living
  • Respect privacy: nothing leaves the group without asking
  • Keep Divine Elixir talk about the ritual and how it feels

We never

  • Promise beauty, health or hormone results
  • Say a product cures, treats or prevents anything
  • Compare women, brands or bodies
  • Talk about weight loss or diets
  • Say anything we cannot back up

If someone is struggling

We are not therapists, and we do not diagnose. If a woman shares something heavy:

  • Reply with warmth, in private if possible.
  • Gently suggest someone she trusts, her doctor or a local support service.
  • If she may be in danger, encourage her to contact local emergency services.
  • Tell the founders, and keep her story private.

Running it12

The first thirty days.

Before launch

  • Create the community and the chat group
  • Add the icon and the description
  • Pin the house rules
  • Write and review weeks 2 to 4
  • Set reminders for every post
  • Invite the first 20 women, people who already love the brand

Every week

  • Monday: schedule the week’s posts
  • Daily: reply and say thank you
  • Friday: note what got the most answers

How we know it is working

  • How many women reply to each post
  • Voice notes and photos shared, which show trust
  • Which topics get the most answers, so we do more of them
  • Women joining our email list from the group
  • The words women use, which we borrow for the brand

After month one

Read the answers, choose four new themes from the bank, and keep the rhythm. The pillars and the days stay the same.
