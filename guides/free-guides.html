Good to know

03

Questions, answered simply.

Is it really free?
Yes. Both guides are free when you join our email list. There is nothing to buy.
Do I need to own Divine Elixir?
No. The guides are for any woman who wants a calmer daily ritual. If you do have Divine Elixir, Your Divine Ritual shows you how to build a moment around it.
What will you do with my email?
We will send you occasional letters from Une Femme Divine. You can unsubscribe at any time, and we never share your email with anyone.
Is my planner information private?
Yes. Your dates, moods and notes stay in your own browser, on your own device. We never see them.
Is this medical advice?
No. The guides share lifestyle and self-care inspiration only. If something feels unusual or worries you, please speak with a doctor.
Do I get the PDFs and the online versions?
Both. The online guides are interactive, with a ritual timer and calendar reminders. The PDFs are made for printing or filling in on your screen.
