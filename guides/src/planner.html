Your calendar

Bring your seasons into your calendar

Each season becomes an all-day event, so your week always shows what you are moving through. Choose how it appears, then send it to the calendar you already use.

How to import the file

Apple Calendar: open the .ics file and choose a calendar. Pick a new one so you can hide or delete it easily.

Google Calendar: on a computer, open Settings, then Import & export, and upload the file. Or add single seasons with the buttons on the right.

Outlook: File, Open & Export, Import/Export, then choose an iCalendar (.ics) file.

The file is a snapshot of today’s dates. If your cycle shifts, update the date above and export again. Delete the earlier calendar first so nothing doubles up.
