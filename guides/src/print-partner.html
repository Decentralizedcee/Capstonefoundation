Rhythm of the Body05

The free
planner.

Her month has a rhythm. Some days ask for rest, others for movement, connection or order. Our free planner helps her notice it and shape her days around it.

We borrow the language of the seasons: an inner winter, spring, summer and autumn. They are invitations, never rules. Every body is different.

Days shown are an example: a 28-day cycle with a 5-day period.

Online

Interactive planner

Her month in colour, a daily log of energy and mood, and a calendar export to Google, Apple or Outlook. Everything stays on her own device.

On paper

Printable PDF

A fillable A4 edition with season guides, a day-by-day log and a calendar page. The same planner, made for the desk or the bag.
