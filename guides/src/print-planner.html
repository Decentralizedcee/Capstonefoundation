Your body keeps a rhythm. Some days ask for rest, others for movement, connection or order. This planner helps you notice it, and shape your days around it.

We borrow the language of the seasons: an inner winter, spring, summer and autumn. Each one brings its own invitations for how to move, what to eat and how to take your ritual. They are invitations, never rules. Every body is different.

Days shown are for a 28-day cycle with a 5-day period. Your own seasons are on the next page.
