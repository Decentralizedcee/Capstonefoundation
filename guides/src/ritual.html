    Good to know

    09

    Questions,
    answered simply.

    What is Divine Elixir?
    A daily collagen ritual, created for women. A carefully composed formula, halal and made in Europe. We present it as a moment of self-care, never as a quick fix.
    How do I take it?
    One scoop a day, stirred into 200–300 ml of water, juice or your morning smoothie until fully dissolved. Each pouch holds 30 scoops, exactly one month. Pregnant, breastfeeding or taking medication? Consult your doctor before use.
    Where is it made?
    Divine Elixir is manufactured in Slovakia, in the European Union, by our partner under European quality standards.
    Is it halal?
    Yes. Divine Elixir is halal. The collagen is bovine and there is no pork.
    Who created Une Femme Divine?
    Two entrepreneurs in Dubai who wanted one daily ritual made truly for women. After a full year of development with a laboratory, our webshop opened on 29 September 2026.
    What results can I expect?
    We do not promise beauty or health results, and we never will. Divine Elixir is a daily ritual and a moment for yourself. How it feels to you is what matters most to us.
