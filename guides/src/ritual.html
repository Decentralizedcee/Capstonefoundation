Welcome

Welcome, you.

Une Femme Divine began in Dubai, with two entrepreneurs and a simple thing we kept noticing. The women around us gave everything to their work, their families and their ambitions. They rarely took anything for themselves.

What was missing was one daily ritual made truly for her. Pure. Honest. Without compromise.

So we did not rush. For a full year we worked with a laboratory on the formula. Testing, refining, testing again, until we stood fully behind it. Divine Elixir is produced by our partner in Europe, in Slovakia, under European quality standards. Our webshop opened on 29 September 2026.

This guide is how we would begin. Slowly, and with you in mind.

With love, the founders of Une Femme Divine

    Good to know

    Questions, answered simply

    What is Divine Elixir?
    A daily collagen ritual, created for women. A carefully composed formula, halal and made in Europe. We present it as a moment of self-care, never as a quick fix.
    Where is it made?
    Divine Elixir is produced by our partner in Europe, in Slovakia, under European quality standards.
    Is it halal?
    Yes. Divine Elixir is halal.
    Who created Une Femme Divine?
    Two entrepreneurs in Dubai who wanted one daily ritual made truly for women. After a full year of work with a laboratory on the formula, we launched our webshop on 29 September 2026.
    How do I take it?
    Follow the preparation and serving instructions printed on your pack. This guide helps you build the moment around it.
    What results can I expect?
    We do not promise beauty or health results, and we never will. Divine Elixir is a daily ritual and a moment for yourself. How it feels to you is what matters most to us.
